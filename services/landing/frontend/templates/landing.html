Operational Architecture

Built for Tactical Reliability & Provenance

Every reconnaissance vector is decoupled from browser lifecycle, maintaining forensic data isolation and zero operational footprint.

Passive & Zero-Footprint Reconnaissance

Designed for zero-trace discovery with automated user-agent rotation, proxy cycling, and rate-limit evasion measures across public surface targets.

Asynchronous Subprocess Daemons

Background workers run independently of active browser sessions, ensuring that long-running deep surface crawls persist and resume flawlessly.

Forensic-Grade Evidence Artifacts

Automated structured exports in CSV, JSON, Markdown, and TXT formats equipped with cryptographic provenance hashes ready for legal and intelligence audits.
